# Cross-functional Role Onboarding Checklist

Use this checklist to ensure new personas/roles are fully integrated into your project team and processes.

- [ ] Role added to project documentation and communication channels
- [ ] Responsibilities clarified and communicated to all team members
- [ ] Key collaboration/interactions mapped with existing roles
- [ ] Onboarding session held to explain workflows, tools, and expectations
- [ ] Success criteria and metrics for the role established
- [ ] Feedback mechanism defined for role effectiveness and improvements

_This checklist supports repeatable, consistent onboarding for new cross-functional roles, improving clarity and accountability._
